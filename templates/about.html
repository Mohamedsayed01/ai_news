About SnapNews

What is SnapNews?

SnapNews is a modern AI-powered news platform that collects real-time headlines from the world's most trusted sources and transforms them into clear, concise professional summaries — in seconds.

Our Mission

In today's fast-paced world, people don't have time to read long articles. Our mission is to make quality journalism accessible and time-efficient for everyone — professionals, students, and curious readers alike.
